# SWAFarmOS

**Smart & Wealth Agriculture Farm Operating System**

SWAFarmOS is an open-core operating system for integrated farm and agribusiness operations.

## Vision

Build a practical system that connects field operations, production batches, inventory, finance, sales, analytics, and decision support in one coherent platform.

## First Reference Deployment

SWAFarmOS will first be developed and validated through a real-world 19-month agribusiness program beginning 1 September 2026, with a target of reaching Rp500 million in measurable accumulated business value/cash-equivalent.

The business program is a reference implementation and test environment for SWAFarmOS. The product itself is designed to remain broadly applicable to farms and agribusinesses.

## Product Principles

- Business requirements drive the software.
- Data before dashboards.
- Every production batch is an economic unit.
- Measure before scaling.
- Modular and API-first architecture.
- Open-source core with a clear path to commercial modules.
- Use third-party software compliantly; do not obscure required notices or attribution.

## Initial Domains

- Farm and land management
- Crop production
- Aquaculture
- Poultry and livestock
- Inventory and inputs
- Harvest and post-harvest
- Sales and customers
- Costs, revenue, cashflow, and profitability
- KPI and unit economics
- Experiments and operational decisions
